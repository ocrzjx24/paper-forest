It is a beautiful spring afternoon. The sky stretches wide and blue, littered with the faintest traces of clouds. The grass shines emerald, a rarity in the parched lands of California, wavering in the light breeze as sunlight glints off its shifting blades.

The boy stands himself up and rushes over to the playground where his friends await, mischievous smiles plastered over their faces. They spin and sprint in joyous circles, in mock firefights and earnest wrestling.
