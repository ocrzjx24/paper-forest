sandstone

somewhere, sometime, far away

  Caelen clambered onto the rock, grunting as he pulled himself up. He paused for a moment to catch his breath, the wind tugging gently at his cloak.
  He shifted to the side as he heard Edel's boots scrape against the stone. She groaned and flopped down stomach-first beside him, arms splayed wide.
  "If this rock were any higher," she puffed, "I would've died." She rolled onto her back with eyes closed.
  Caelen chuckled. "Dramatic."
  He took in the view that laid before them. A quaint little bridge of golden sandstone, its ancient arches draped in moss and ivy. The river beneath it shimmered in the noonday sun, while verdant trees bowed at the edges, swaying in the wind as if greeting the band of travelers. In the distance, silver mountains and cumulus clouds.
  "You folks tired already?"
  Caelen and Edel turned to find their third companion jovially munching away on a piece of flatbread.
  "Of course you're not, Yuvo, you big beast," Edel retorted. Yuvo shrugged, setting his gargantuan bag of equipment down beside them.
  The trio settled into a comfortable silence. A breeze moved through the leaves behind them, carrying with it many a sweet scent. They hadn't known such peaceful respite in a long time. For once, the blades at their sides could remain sheathed, untouched, forgotten.
  Caelen eventually broke the silence. "We ought to get moving again soon."
  "Aye. It's nice here, though. And safe," Yuvo added, his voice muffled with bread.
  Edel smiled and turned to look at Caelen. "We've been on the move for ages. I'm sure you're not opposed to a little rest, Cay," she said.
  Caelen considered this for a moment before giving a small nod. He eased himself against a nearby tree, letting his eyes fall shut.

"JUST FOR A MOMENT, THEN."
