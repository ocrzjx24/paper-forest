sandstone

somewhere, sometime, far away

Caelus clambered onto the rock, grunting as he pulled himself up. He paused for a moment to catch his breath, the wind tugging gently at his cloak.

He shifted to the side as he heard Laine's boots scrape against the stone. She groaned and flopped down beside him, arms splayed wide.

"If this rock were any higher, I would've died," she muttered, eyes closed.

Caelus chuckled. "Dramatic."

He took in the view that laid before them. A quaint little bridge of golden sandstone, its ancient arches draped in moss and ivy. The river beneath it shimmered in the noonday sun, while verdant trees bowed at the edges, swaying in the wind as if beckoning the band of travelers.

"You folks tired already?"

Caelus and Laine turned to find their third companion jovially munching away on a piece of flatbread.

"Are you not, Yuvo?" Laine retorted. Yuvo shrugged, setting his gargantuan bag of equipment down beside them.

The trio settled into a comfortable silence. A breeze moved through the leaves behind them, carrying with it many a sweet scent. They hadn't known such peaceful respite in a long time. For once, the blades at their sides could remain sheathed, untouched, forgotten.

Caelus eventually broke the silence. "We ought to get moving again soon."

"Yeah. It's nice here, though. And safe," Yuvo added, his voice muffled with bread.

Laine smiled. "I'm not opposed to a little rest."

Caelus considered this for a moment, then nodded. He looked out across the river. He and his companions had traveled this beautiful world. Did they once stop to admire it?

"THEN LET US REST."
