[EXCERPT FROM AUDIO LOG #4, MAY 29 2044]

妈 said these mulberries were nothing like the ones she had as a kid. These freaked me out, 'cause they kinda looked like caterpillars. [chuckles] Meanwhile, 妈 said the ones in China were more blackberry-esque.

I remember they were sweet, just like, straight-up sugar. The ones in China supposedly had a tanginess to them. And I remember 妈 would always go on about how Chinese fruits were better, but… well, she always said that, no matter what. [laughs]
