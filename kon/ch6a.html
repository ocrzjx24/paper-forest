mulberry jam

just slightly too sour

In my eyes, the homemade-ness kinda makes it better. Not that it's sweeter or smoother — honestly, it was always a little too sour or uneven. Stems still floating here and there. [laughs] But it was ours.

It ain't Costco jam. Which, maybe objectively tastes better. But we stained our hands and had a damn good time making this stuff. It doesn't care where we came from. You offer me Costco, or you offer me this slightly-too-sour, stem-filled, watery jam — I'd take this one any day.

I remember my allergies were killing me. The weather was great, but I was sneezing, like, every minute. But my mom, she just hands me the berry picker and tells me to stop complaining. [laughs]
