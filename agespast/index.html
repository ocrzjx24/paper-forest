forever and again
rolling hills and white concrete
can you ever recall
who you used to be?

some things belong to
ages past
to be fondly forgotten

we don't remember being
here, yet here we were

waylaid memories, static emotions

whatever kept the world turning
is now lost to
ages past

and i'm sorry for ( )
but there's no point in dwelling

and then i feel homesick
for a home i never had

so within unmade decisions of
ages past
dip your hand into the water

ages past
make life worth living.

i have a terrible habit
of lingering in the past.
doors close and lock themselves,
yet i still knock.
i curiously gaze through the glass,
window-shopping for things
time can no longer afford.
my focus slips
and i catch myself staring back.
i shatter the glass.

i wake up every day
with a thousand dreams.
nine hundred slip away
before my feet fall out of bed.
fifty rinse out with
the foam of my toothpaste.
twenty-five take the bus with me,
then disembark somewhere i don’t.
fifteen flutter at the edges
of my consciousness,
then fly away mid-lecture.

ten, nine, eight, seven, six
five, four, three, two, one.
one follows me back to the bus,
back home, back to bed,
guardian angel.

i make promises to myself,
but memories i swore
i’d never forget
disappear as unceremoniously
as a grocery store receipt.
they sediment into the ground.
i walk on ages past.

about

"a collection of pieces that have no home. revisited, remastered, rearranged, and remembered. 2021-2025"

One of my favorite pastimes is editing random pics my friends and I take using Photoshop. Sometimes, they'd be part of a themed set or collection, but a lot of the time, they were these one-off pieces chock full of words that meant nothing and waxed poetic without any real substance. When digging back through my files, I realized that a lot of these pieces carried with them memories and weight that I had forgotten. So, to me, "ages past" is about uncovering memories of youth and friendship, reflecting on them, and setting them free.
