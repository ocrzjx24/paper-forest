mulberry jam

just slightly too sour

[EXCERPT FROM AUDIO LOG #4 - MAY 11, 2045]

Mom said these mulberries were nothing like the ones she had as a kid. These were like sweet little caterpillars. The ones in China were more like blackberries.

And 'cause the mulberries were too sweet on their own, we'd combine them with these strawberries that we had, strawberries that were too sour, and we'd make jam.

I don't know if we ever got it right. But there's a charm to that. Isn't there?
