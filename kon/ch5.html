page from a traveler's notebook

origin unknown

april 31, 1999

mist makes me think of a more refreshing cold. fog feels unknown. mysterious. where does the dew come from? it glistens like tiny diamonds in the morning sun. does its heritage lie in the dense clusters of fog, or the fleeting breath of mist?
to me, dew is fog come to rest after a long morning of flitting about. mist may be too feeble to set its roots down on the leaves of trees. fog, however, moves from confusion to certainty. vapor to drop. translucent as a child, then refractive as a parent. mist can never be that. it does not carry the strong emotions of fog or quiet wisdom of dew. mist can only exist as a moment, it cannot transmute. its life cycle is far too short.
it disappears, leaving behind a cold damp. the dew drop's life cycle continues. relative humidity increases and it fades. slowly, certainly. not into the unknown, but into the unseen. maybe it shall return for another journey. maybe it sets alight on a blade of grass.
or maybe it's never to be seen again.
