do you remember me?

The sun sits lazily in the sky, draping long purple shadows atop the sidewalk. A teenager waltzes beneath the canopy of the gazebo and sets himself down on the benches, sliding the backpack off his shoulders. His movements are unhurried, unbothered.

He tousles his jet black hair and fixes the glasses slipping down his sweaty nose. He rummages through his bag and retrieves a granola bar, reclining and munching away.

Without warning, a tickle rises in his throat, and he sneezes — once, twice, thrice. He coughs and sighs.
