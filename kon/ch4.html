brother

blades of steel lain in blades of grass

we enter with heads high and chests puffed, ready for war
each step a step closer to death. we stare it down.
we glare and jeer and taunt

love and hate and anger and joy and misery and terror
and destruction and creation and hope and confusion
and acceptance and desperation
and everything
endlessly, endlessly, endlessly running

from what and to where, we do not know
an infinity of things we do not know
for we are foolish

light scattering until it breaks into little pieces. we flutter down this freeway
with the vibrancy of the void in our faces, and the prismatic breeze in our hair
fighting this war for millennia until it becomes calming
the pearl, the anchor, the star

FLY AWAY, FLY AWAY
