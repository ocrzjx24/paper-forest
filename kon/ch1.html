before i was born

...

I was adrift in ether. Aimless.
I was an arrow waiting to be nocked. I sought the welcoming hands of the void.
Drawn to the center, waiting to be drawn. To be given direction.
The void sighed.

They whisper into my ear from lightyears away. I search, and search, but I cannot find them. They call my name, or names; they invite memories of pasts and futures I have already lived.
I search, and search.

Trenches so deep, they would go beyond the core of the Earth.
Mountains so tall, they would reach beyond the atmosphere.
There is no such thing as time.

I will swim through the emptiness in slow motion. The void will continue to beckon me.
I will search, and search.
I will enter their cold embrace and be released into a world that cares not for me.
It loves me, it hates me.

This dream had yet to end. The next is waiting to begin.
