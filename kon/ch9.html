after i was dead

...

My life was fulfilling. I did everything I wanted to do. One of these is a lie.
When the void took aim, they were perfect. When I was released, I was imperfect.

I sliced through the universe like a blade through silk. Ripping fabric, tearing threads.
I tried to tie them up as I flew past, but I was too swift for my own good.
Where do I go? I searched, and searched.

The quiver of the void awaits me, and I am aware. A full circle. It was always in the plan.
This life was recorded, written down onto infinite paper, woven into myself.
A receipt for the lives I had checked out.
Motes of dust.

The void.
I entered their cold embrace and was released into a world that cared not for me.
It loved me, it hated me.
It nourished me, it killed me.
It returned me to the void. The only one willing to accept.

This dream had yet to end. The next is waiting to begin.
