about

"king of nothing" was created for a project during my time at UC Davis. A lot of these pieces were written very last-minute at first, and I recall even submitting the project a couple hours late, haha... but I eventually went back and refined everything, and here we are.

I wanted to write something that highlighted themes of warped nostalgia, personal growth, and living in the present, using different writing styles to reach into different corners of all those ideas. I want to give a big thanks to Rahul, Kyle, and Cam for writing a chapter each; it was lovely seeing and deciphering what they came up with.
