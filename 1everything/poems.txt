title: before i was born
set: king of nothing
info: update later
---
I was adrift in ether. Aimless.
I was an arrow waiting to be nocked. I sought the welcoming hands of the void.
Drawn to the center, waiting to be drawn. To be given direction.
The void sighed.

They whisper into my ear from lightyears away. I search, and search, but I cannot find them. They call my name, or names; they invite memories of pasts and futures I have already lived.
I search, and search.

Trenches so deep, they would go beyond the core of the Earth.
Mountains so tall, they would reach beyond the atmosphere.
There is no such thing as time.

I will swim through the emptiness in slow motion. The void will continue to beckon me.
I will search, and search.
I will enter their cold embrace and be released into a world that cares not for me.
It loves me, it hates me.

This dream had yet to end. The next is waiting to begin.

Beneath the snow white gazebo, nestled away in a shady corner, lies a young bespectacled boy. His hair stands in spiky tufts, and his navy blue jacket is dusted in tanbark. He sits up and wipes his nose before succumbing to a flurry of three sneezes.

---

The sun sits lazily in the sky, draping long purple shadows atop the sidewalk. A teenager waltzes beneath the canopy of the gazebo and sets himself down on the benches, sliding the backpack off his shoulders. His movements are unhurried, unbothered.

He tousles his jet black hair and fixes the glasses slipping down his sweaty nose. He rummages through his bag and retrieves a granola bar, reclining and munching away.

Without warning, a tickle rises in his throat, and he sneezes — once, twice, thrice. He coughs and sighs.

---

The young man says his goodbyes, sauntering away from the house and hopping into the driver seat. He adjusts the rearview mirror, catching a glimpse of himself for a moment.

His acne is worse than ever. His hair is overgrown, with what was once a sharp mullet now spilling down to his shoulders. Though he maintains his ritual of shaving every morning, the shadow along his jaw has begun to darken earlier in the day.

title: page from a traveler's notebook
set: king of nothing
info: update later
---
(written by Rahul)

mist makes me think of a more refreshing cold. fog feels unknown. mysterious. where does the dew come from? it glistens like tiny diamonds in the morning sun. does its heritage lie in the dense clusters of fog, or the fleeting breath of mist?
to me, dew is fog come to rest after a long morning of flitting about. mist may be too feeble to set its roots down on the leaves of trees. fog, however, moves from confusion to certainty. vapor to drop. translucent as a child, then refractive as a parent. mist can never be that. it does not carry the strong emotions of fog or quiet wisdom of dew. mist can only exist as a moment, it cannot transmute. its life cycle is far too short.
it disappears, leaving behind a cold damp. the dew drop's life cycle continues. relative humidity increases and it fades. slowly, certainly. not into the unknown, but into the unseen. maybe it shall return for another journey. maybe it sets alight on a blade of grass.
or maybe it's never to be seen again.
